Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Worn does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.

Your Photos and Closet

Outfit photos, the pieces cut out of them, ratings, dates, notes, prices, brands, saved looks, plans, packing lists and wishlist entries are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. Cutting people and clothes out of photos and suggesting a clothing category are done on your device with Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

If your device backs up to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup, under Apple's terms and your own settings. Deleting the app, or choosing "Erase all outfits and pieces" in Worn's settings, removes the data from your device.

Camera and Photo Library

Worn uses your camera only when you choose to take a photo. When you pick from your library, you use Apple's photo picker, which gives Worn only the photo you choose. Worn does not ask for access to your photo library.

Location and Weather

If you allow it, Worn uses your approximate location to get today's weather from Apple's WeatherKit, so it can suggest outfits you wore at a similar temperature. Your location is sent to Apple only to get the weather and is handled under Apple's privacy policy. Worn does not store your location; only the temperature is saved with an outfit. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings and Worn will still work.

Reminders

If you turn on the morning reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS. Nothing about it is sent to the developer.

Subscriptions

Worn Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles your payment details and the developer never sees them. The app only checks with StoreKit on your device whether your subscription is active.

Third-Party Services

Worn does not use advertising services, tracking technologies, analytics platforms, or third-party marketing tools. WeatherKit and the App Store are operated by Apple, which processes data according to Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Worn does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Worn's features change. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
