Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Memento is a daily memory journal. It lets you save a photo or video from each day, add an optional note and mood, and browse your saved days as a timeline or an illustrated path.

Information We Collect

Memento does not collect any personal information. Everything you create in the app — your photos and videos, notes, moods, and dates — is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server, and Memento does not sell or share personal information.

Profile details you choose to enter (such as a display name, username, or email address) are stored locally on your device and are only shown back to you inside the app.

Photos and Videos

Memento uses the system photo picker so you can choose the photos and videos you want to save. The app only receives the items you select. Copies of those items are saved inside the app on your device so your memories stay intact even if the originals are later removed from your library. They are never sent anywhere.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, Memento schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder settings never leave your device.

Third-Party Services

Memento does not use accounts, advertising services, tracking technologies, analytics platforms, or third-party marketing tools.

Children's Privacy

Memento does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Because Memento does not collect personal information on any server, the app does not maintain a database of user information. Your memories are protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode and Face ID. Users are responsible for securing their device and Apple account.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any memory from within the app. Deleting the app removes all of its locally stored data, including saved photos, videos, and notes. If your device is included in an iCloud or computer backup, Apple's backup may contain a copy of the app's data until that backup is replaced or deleted.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Memento's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
