Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Tracely does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code.

Camera

Tracely uses your camera only to show a live view on screen while you trace. The camera feed is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Photos

When you choose a photo to trace, you pick it with Apple's photo picker, which gives Tracely only that one photo. It is used on your device while you trace and is not saved by the app or sent anywhere. Tracely does not ask for access to your photo library.

Purchases

Tracely Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees or stores your payment details; the app only learns from Apple whether you own Tracely Pro.

Third-Party Services

Tracely does not use advertising services, tracking technologies, analytics platforms, or third-party marketing tools. The App Store is operated by Apple, which processes data according to Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

Tracely does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.
