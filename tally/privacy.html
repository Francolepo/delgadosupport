Tally Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Tally is a personal budgeting app by Francisco Delgado ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Tally. The short version: your money data stays on your device, and I don't collect it.

What I collect

Nothing. Tally has no accounts on my servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. I do not receive, store, or sell your transactions, budgets, or any other financial information.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in Tally (transactions, jars, budgets, bills, and settings) is stored locally on your device. Tally works without an internet connection.

Optional iCloud sync

If you choose to sign in with Apple inside Tally, your data syncs between your own devices through your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. That data is stored by Apple and is only accessible to you. I cannot see it. Tally keeps your first name (if Apple provides it) on your device to greet you. You can turn sync off in Settings.

Subscriptions and payments

Tally offers a subscription with a free trial. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Tally schedules reminders on your device, for example a reminder before your free trial ends. These are local notifications and are not sent through my servers.

Wallet and Shortcuts

If you set up the optional Shortcuts automation, purchase details you choose to send from Apple Wallet are passed to Tally on your device. They are not sent anywhere else.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your transactions as a CSV file from Settings and delete all of your data at any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. If you used iCloud sync, you can also remove Tally's data in your device's iCloud settings.

Children

Tally is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the effective date above.
